# 02 · Research Foundations

Atlas was designed research-first, not from opinion. Every major design choice traces to a finding
below. Sources are linked; content is paraphrased for licensing compliance.

---

## Why capture alone isn't enough — a plan is what frees the mind
The Zeigarnik effect: unfinished tasks nag at attention. But Masicampo & Baumeister (2011) showed
the nagging stops not when the task is *done* but when it has a **specific plan** — a plan produces
the same relief in working memory as completion.
→ **Design impact:** capturing a note isn't enough; Atlas must attach a *when/how* (an
implementation-intention trigger), or the mind keeps carrying the load.
[Masicampo & Baumeister 2011 (PDF)](https://opis-cdn.tinkoffjournal.ru/mercury/MasicampoBaumeister2011JPSP.pdf)

## If-then plans are among the strongest behavior levers known
Implementation intentions ("after X, I'll do Y") show a large pooled effect across 94 studies.
→ **Design impact:** the allocator attaches a trigger to tasks, not just a slot.
[Meta-analysis (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, PDF)](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Paschal_Sheeran/publication/37367696_Implementation_intentions_and_goal_achievement__a_meta-analysis_of_effects_and_processes/links/0fcfd504f109545e51000000.pdf) ·
[summary](https://www.thebehavioralscientist.com/glossary/implementation-intentions)

## Cognitive offloading works — but only if you trust the store
Externalising tasks improves thinking; whether people offload depends on their **confidence in the
external store**.
→ **Design impact:** instant confirmation + always keep the original audio. Trust is a feature; if
it breaks once, the owner goes back to holding everything in his head.
[Nature HSS review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-026-06621-5) ·
[metamemory & offloading](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6838677/)

## Running many ventures taxes attention non-linearly
Portfolio operators launch more but their ventures are **less likely to survive**; the academic
frame is literally the *allocation of entrepreneurial attention*.
→ **Design impact:** Atlas's core job is allocation, not storage.
[Portfolio entrepreneurs (Management Science)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/epdf/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3929) ·
[allocation of attention](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/222314895_Allocation_of_Entrepreneurial_Attention)

## Context-switching is the tax, and it compounds
~23 minutes to refocus after an interruption; effort per project collapses as projects multiply
(2 projects → ~40% each, 3 → ~20%, 4 → ~10%). Sophie Leroy's "attention residue" explains the drag
of switching before a loop is closed.
→ **Design impact:** batch by domain; a quick "where I left off" note parks a loop cleanly (that's
also the journaling).
[Cost of context switching](https://www.rock.so/blog/cost-of-context-switching) ·
[compounding](https://contextcost.com/) ·
[Leroy, attention residue](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/pdf/10.1287/orsc.2017.1184)

## A list has no floor; a calendar does — timeboxing beats listing
Timeboxing ranked the single most useful productivity practice in a survey of 100; a list grows
without limit, a finite calendar cannot.
→ **Design impact:** Atlas confronts tasks with the day's *real* hours instead of an endless list.
[HBR](https://hbr.org/2018/12/how-timeboxing-works-and-why-it-will-make-you-more-productive) ·
[why finite beats open-ended](https://www.ixcoach.com/practices/parkinsons-law/time-boxing-tasks)

## The planning fallacy is universal and predictable
People underestimate task duration even knowing past tasks ran long; implementation intentions
reduce it.
→ **Design impact:** the Ledger learns the owner's *real* durations, so "quick" stops being a lie.
[The Planning Fallacy](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/251449615_The_Planning_Fallacy)

## Goal conflict harms through ambivalence, not the conflict itself
With many domains, goals compete — but the damage runs through **ambivalence / mixed emotions**,
not the raw conflict; when goals *facilitate* each other, wellbeing rises.
→ **Design impact:** the brief names the trade-off out loud ("2h to trading means deen gets 0"),
turning low-grade guilt into a clean decision.
[Goal conflict & wellbeing (review)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/277338574_Goal_conflict_and_well-being_A_review_and_hierarchical_model_of_goal_conflict_ambivalence_self-discrepancy_and_self-concordance) ·
[indirect effect via mixed emotion](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.653512/full)

## Manage energy, not just time; align hard work to your peak
Time is finite but energy renews across body/emotions/mind/spirit; matching demanding work to your
chronotype's peak is a zero-cost performance lever (morning peak → deep work early).
→ **Design impact:** the allocator is morning-peak aware — deep work AM, admin in the afternoon dip.
[Manage Your Energy, Not Your Time (HBR)](https://hbr.org/2007/10/manage-your-energy-not-your-time) ·
[chronotype scheduling](https://mindlabneuroscience.com/chronotype-science-optimizing-brain/)

## Self-monitoring changes behavior on its own
Simply tracking a behavior reliably shifts it; interstitial journaling folds notes, tasks, and
time-tracking into one stream.
→ **Design impact:** journaling isn't a bolt-on — it's the same voice pipe, and it powers duration
learning + domain-attention tracking.
[Self-monitoring meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31409357/) ·
[interstitial journaling](https://nesslabs.com/interstitial-journaling)

## Relationships are a domain that dies silently — and they extend life
Close ties are among the strongest predictors of health; a good friend network raises life
expectancy; Dunbar's ~5/15/50/150 tiers each need different contact frequency.
→ **Design impact:** the People organ tracks the inner circle and nudges when a close tie goes
quiet, exactly like a starving venture.
[Friendship & health (Dunbar)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11918532/) ·
[tiers & cadence](https://rolodexcrm.com/blog/beyond-150-beat-dunbars-ceiling-with-systems-not-hustle)

## Humans thrive on autonomy, competence, relatedness (SDT)
Wellbeing rises when these three needs are met.
→ **Design impact:** Atlas **advises and names trade-offs — the owner decides** (autonomy), shows
growth/wins not just backlog (competence), and holds the People organ (relatedness). A tyrant tool
gets abandoned.
[Self-Determination Theory](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11392867/)

## Reviews are the highest-leverage habit — and the one everyone skips
The weekly review is called the "critical success factor"; people skip it because a messy list
turns review into guilt. Start at 15 minutes.
→ **Design impact:** Atlas pre-digests the brief so review is clarity, not guilt; and it introduces
reviews *gently* (the owner is new to them), celebratory-first.
[Why the review matters](https://gettingthingsdone.com/2018/08/episode-43-the-power-of-the-gtd-weekly-review/) ·
[why people avoid it](https://developers.fabric.so/learn/weekly-review)

## A fully-automatic system becomes a graveyard — keep one friction point
The Bullet Journal's "migration" (rewriting tasks by hand) lets stale things die; that friction is
a feature. And productivity apps have brutal retention (~4% by day 30) — the killer is maintenance.
→ **Design impact:** keep one weekly friction moment; and Atlas must do useful work while the owner
is absent, or it dies like the rest.
[Bullet Journal method](https://bulletjournal.com/blogs/faq/what-is-the-bullet-journal-method) ·
[why productivity apps die](https://firmsideai.substack.com/p/why-productivity-apps-die)

## The Arabic-voice reality (a technical constraint, not a nice-to-have)
Egyptian-dialect speech recognition has high word-error-rate (~25–30% for open models; the pretty
single-digit numbers are read Modern Standard Arabic, not dialect). The owner's exact register —
Egyptian + English tech terms — has purpose-built models, but transcripts will still be noisy.
→ **Design impact:** treat the transcript as a *noisy signal* — correct by meaning using the
owner's domains + names as context, echo back a confirmation line, and **always keep the original
audio**.
[Cohere Arabic WER](https://huggingface.co/blog/CohereLabs/cohere-transcribe-arabic-07-2026-release) ·
[Egyptian-tuned Whisper](https://huggingface.co/MightyStudent/whisper-large-v3-arabic) ·
[code-switched Egyptian+English model](https://huggingface.co/Seif-Eldeen-Sameh/whisper-medium-arabic-codeswitched)
